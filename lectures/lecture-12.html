Lecture 12 — The frontier that moves: artificial intelligence, China and us

Slides (online)Slides (PDF)

Wednesday 25 November 2026, online on Teams · Block: innovation

Questions

  • Is artificial intelligence intelligent? Which definition of intelligence fits this moment?
  • What is AI made of, and how did China catch up with the United States despite the chip controls?
  • Why do American firms sell closed models, while Chinese firms give theirs away?
  • What happens to the first rung of the job ladder, in America and in China?

Key concepts

The Turing test · intelligence as skill, or as the speed of learning a new skill · benchmarks that move · chips and electricity · export controls · distance to the frontier (lecture 10) · closed and open models · rents and spillovers (lectures 10-11) · complements · entry-level jobs.

In class

  1. The end of lecture 11: the second half of "Ray of Hope?", from the cities to China as a whole.
  2. What is intelligence? Kasparov and Deep Blue, an anthill, three definitions, and an answer.
  3. The physics, and China's chase: electricity, the chip controls of 7 October 2022, DeepSeek, the chip that went back and forth, how close China is.
  4. Two ways to sell intelligence: closed in America, open in China, read with the economics of innovation.
  5. The first rung: a call centre, fewer first jobs for the young, China's graduates.
  6. After the break: the practice test and its debrief.
